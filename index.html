PARMCO

An iPhone interface for controlling a stepper motor over Bluetooth, with independent measurements of speed and shaft position.

Explore the system ↓

01 / System

From the phone to the motor

The iPhone sends commands over Bluetooth Low Energy. The Raspberry Pi interprets them and drives the stepper motor through the SN754410NE driver. The 4N25 provides the driver’s enable signal.

02 / Checkpoints

Build progress

CP1

Keyboard motor control

Built the driver circuit and controlled the stepper from the Pi keyboard.

CP2

Bluetooth and IR speed sensing

Tested the iPhone-to-Pi connection. The Pi displayed app commands and sent acknowledgments back; motor control still ran from the keyboard. The IR sensor provided a measured RPM for comparison with blade counts.

CP3 · Complete

App control and shaft position

The iPhone app controls the motor over Bluetooth and displays live speed and shaft angle. It also supports zero calibration and angle targets.

Final · In progress

Feedback and validation

We’re working on speed and position feedback, then we’ll run the final tests and document the measured results.
